About biitle.nl


biitle.nl was created for a product that was going to be called the "biitle", a touch-screen clock with extra functionality, however, that never became a real product.

Now, biitle.nl creates open-source software and also develops ppddvv, a paid POS-system that works with the open-source FluidServer POS server.

We believe in right to repair and if we ever try to make a physical product, we will release schematics and repair guides.

This domain was registered in the 29th of March, 2023, after a few weeks of thinking about the idea. The name biitle.nl is a combination of beetle and bit. .nl and .de were the cheapest TLDs, however, .nl fit better, so we used that instead of .de.
